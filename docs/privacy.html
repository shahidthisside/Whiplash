Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short: Whiplash has no servers, ads or tracking. If you turn on sync, your library is saved in your own Google Drive, and the developer can never see it.

Whiplash is a free, open-source Android music player. It has no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains what the app does with your information.

Without signing in

Everything you create in Whiplash (playlists, favourites, history, downloads and settings) is stored only on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Optional Google sign-in and sync

If you choose Settings › Account & sync › Sign in with Google, Whiplash asks Google for these permissions:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (email, profile), shown on your profile in Settings.
  • A private app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Whiplash can only see files it created in that hidden folder. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Whiplash saves one small file in that folder containing your playlists, favourites, listening history, Speed dial, Monthly Replay counts, lyric timing, settings, and the profile name and photo you chose in the app. This is what lets another phone signed in to the same account restore your library.

This file is stored in your own Google Drive and counts towards your Drive storage. It never passes through, and is never stored on, any server run by the developer. The developer cannot see it.

On your phone, Whiplash keeps your email, name and profile picture link so it can show your profile. The access token Google provides is short-lived and is never saved.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Whiplash's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to provide sync and your profile inside the app. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone.

Other services the app talks to

  • YouTube / YouTube Music, to search and play music (through the open-source NewPipeExtractor library). Your requests go directly from your phone to YouTube.
  • LRCLIB and lyrics.ovh, to fetch song lyrics. Only the song's title, artist and length are sent.

These services have their own privacy policies. Whiplash does not send them your Google account details.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Account & sync › Delete synced data removes the file from your Drive and signs you out.
  • In Google Drive: Settings › Manage apps › Whiplash › Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove access: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Whiplash.
  • On your phone: uninstalling Whiplash, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it stored on the device.

Signing out, or turning off Account & sync, never deletes anything.

Children

Whiplash is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
