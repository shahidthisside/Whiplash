Whiplash

A free, open-source Android music player for YouTube Music and the songs on your phone.

  • Stream and download music, with synced lyrics
  • Playlists, favourites, Speed dial and Monthly Replay
  • Optional Google sign-in to sync your library between devices, stored in your own Google Drive
  • No ads, no tracking, no accounts required
